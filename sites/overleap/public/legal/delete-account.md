**Last updated: 2026-10**

This page explains how to delete your Overleap account, and what happens to your data afterwards. Overleap is operated by Overleap LLC.

### 1. Delete from inside the app (recommended)

1. Open the Overleap app and go to **Account** (in the bottom navigation bar on phones, in the sidebar on desktop).
2. Make sure you are signed in — your email address is shown on the page.
3. Scroll to the bottom of the page and tap **Delete Account**.
4. Tap **Confirm** in the dialogue that appears.

Deletion takes effect immediately; no manual processing is involved. You are signed out automatically.

### 2. If you have already uninstalled the app

Email privacy@overleap.io with the subject **Delete account**, and state the email address you registered with.

So that nobody can have your account deleted by impersonating you, we send a confirmation message to the registered address; deletion goes ahead once you reply to confirm it. We complete the request within 30 days of receiving your confirmation.

### 3. Data that is deleted

Permanently deleted the moment you confirm, with no way to recover it:

- **Login identifiers** — the binding between your email address and the account. The address is released and may be used to register again.
- **Device registrations** — every device registered to your account. All of them lose access immediately.
- **The account itself** — deactivated, and can no longer be used to sign in.

### 4. Data that is retained, and for how long

The following is not removed with the account. Each entry gives the period and the reason:

- **Transaction and order records** — kept for 7 years. Required by financial regulations; we cannot delete these earlier.
- **Subscription consent records** — kept with payment records.
- **Support correspondence** — kept for 2 years after the issue is resolved.
- **Connection logs** (connection timestamps and the server location used — never browsing history) — deleted automatically after 7 days on their normal schedule.
- **Traffic volume statistics** (byte-count totals only, with no record of what was accessed) — deleted automatically after 2 months on their normal schedule.

Apart from the records listed above, remaining account data is erased within 30 days of the deletion request.

### 5. Before you delete

- Unused subscription time is **not** refunded and cannot be restored afterwards. If you're within 14 days of your first payment for a subscription billed by us (not through the App Store), or of an annual renewal, email support@overleap.io before deleting your account to withdraw with a prorated refund (Terms, Section 7).
- Deleting your account does not cancel a renewing subscription. Cancel it first on your account page, or in your App Store settings for iPhone purchases.
- The action cannot be undone. If you only want to change device or change email address, use device management or the change-email feature in the app instead.

### 6. Questions

For data protection questions contact privacy@overleap.io; for anything else contact support@overleap.io.
