## Connecting from China: two different approaches

<strong>Astrill hands you the choice of protocol.</strong> It offers its own OpenWeb and StealthVPN alongside standard protocols such as WireGuard and OpenVPN, and you switch until one works. That suits people who like to tinker and know what to try when something breaks.

<strong>开途 Kaitu has one protocol and does the switching for you.</strong> Its k2 protocol prefers QUIC and falls back to TCP automatically when QUIC is disrupted, on the same port. Its congestion control, k2cc, is built for the high packet loss common on cross-border links, so it doesn't slow down every time a packet is lost. There's nothing to choose: you tap connect.

Neither side promises it will always work. Astrill's refund policy even states: "If our product is not working on your computer because VPN service is blocked by your ISP, we are not responsible."
