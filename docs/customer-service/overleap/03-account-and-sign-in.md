# Account & Sign-in

## Signing in

1. Enter your email address.
2. We email you a 6-digit code — enter it in the app.
3. Your first sign-in creates your account automatically.

You can also set a password later (Account → Set Password) and sign in with email + password.

There is no Google, Apple, or phone-number sign-in.

### The code didn't arrive
1. Check the email address for typos.
2. Check your spam / junk folder.
3. Wait a minute and request a new code.
If it still doesn't arrive, hand off to support.

## Devices

- Up to 5 devices can be signed in at the same time.
- Signing in on a 6th device automatically signs out the device you used least recently.
- See your devices in Account → My Devices.

## Signing out

Account → **Switch Account** signs you out of the app.

## Changing your email

Account → Login Email → **Modify**. (Available to active subscribers.)

## Deleting your account

- **In the app:** Account → scroll to the bottom → **Delete Account** → Confirm. Deletion takes effect immediately.
- **Without the app:** email privacy@overleap.io with the subject "Delete account". It's completed within 30 days of confirmation.

Unused subscription time is not refunded when an account is deleted. Deleting the account does not cancel a renewing subscription: if the user has an active subscription, remind them to cancel it first so they aren't charged again. If they are within 14 days of a payment that may qualify for withdrawal (see Billing & Refunds), they should request the withdrawal before deleting the account.

## Managing your subscription on the web

Sign in at overleap.io/account. The **Subscription** tab has "Manage subscription" (cancel, change card, download invoices). The **Security** tab lets you set or change your password.
