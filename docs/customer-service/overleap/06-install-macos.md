# Installing on macOS

Requirements: macOS 12 (Monterey) or later. Works on both Intel and Apple silicon Macs.

## Steps

1. Open https://overleap.io/install on your Mac and download the macOS installer (file name like `Overleap_0.4.11_universal.pkg`).
2. Double-click the downloaded `.pkg` file.
3. Click **Continue** → **Install**, then enter your Mac password when asked. (This sets up the VPN service.)
4. Overleap opens automatically when installation finishes.
5. Sign in with your email and the code we send you, then click **Connect**.

## "Overleap can't be opened" / blocked by macOS

The installer is signed and notarized by Apple, so this is rare. If it happens:
1. Open **System Settings** → **Privacy & Security**.
2. Scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to the Overleap message.
3. Enter your Mac password.

## The app asks for your Mac password when it starts

If the background service isn't running, Overleap asks for your password once to restore it. This is expected.

## Updates

Overleap updates automatically. The update is applied the next time you quit and reopen the app.
