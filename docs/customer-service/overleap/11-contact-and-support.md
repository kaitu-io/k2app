# Contact & Support

## Ways to reach us

- **In the app (best for problems):** Account → **Feedback**, or the floating **Report Issue** button. Write a short description and tap **Submit Ticket**. App version, system info and diagnostic logs are attached automatically. Replies come by email. You can see your tickets under "My Tickets".
- **Live chat:** on overleap.io (Support, Pricing, and Purchase pages). You can send screenshots (PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, up to 5 MB).
- **Email:** support@overleap.io
- **Privacy requests:** privacy@overleap.io
- **Legal:** legal@overleap.io

We do not offer phone or video calls.

## When to use which

- Connection or speed problem → in-app Feedback (the logs help us find the cause).
- Billing, refunds, account questions → chat or support@overleap.io.
- Account deletion → in the app (Account → Delete Account), or email privacy@overleap.io.
