# Overleap — Product Overview

## What Overleap is

Overleap is a private VPN app. Slogan: "Your browsing is your business."

- **No activity logs.** We keep only your email, your plan, and the devices you are signed in on. Connection records (time and server location) are kept for 7 days; daily data totals are kept for 2 months. We never record which sites you visit.
- **Hides where you go, even from your internet provider.** Overleap uses Encrypted Client Hello, so your provider can't see which site you connect to.
- **Stays fast on poor networks.** Overleap's own rate control (k2cc) keeps speeds up on congested or lossy connections.
- **Seamless network changes.** Moving from Wi-Fi to mobile data doesn't drop the connection.
- **Works while travelling.**
