# iPhone & iPad

Requirements: iOS 16 or later.

## Availability

The Overleap iPhone/iPad app is **coming soon to the App Store**. It is not available to download yet.

If a user asks about iPhone/iPad:
- Tell them the app is coming soon to the App Store.
- They can already subscribe on overleap.io and use Overleap on Windows, Mac, and Android; the same subscription will work on iPhone/iPad when the app is released.
- If they need more detail, hand off to a human.

## Once available

1. Search for **Overleap** in the App Store and install it.
2. Sign in with your email and code.
3. Tap **Connect**. iOS asks "Overleap Would Like to Add VPN Configurations" — tap **Allow**, then confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode.

On iPhone/iPad the Yearly plan can be bought in the app. The Monthly plan is only available on overleap.io.
