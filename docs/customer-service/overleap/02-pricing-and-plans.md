# Plans & Pricing

## Plans

There is one plan, **Overleap Basic**, billed monthly or yearly.

| Billing | USD | GBP | EUR |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly | $11.99 / month | £9.99 / month | €11.99 / month |
| Yearly (best value) | $79 / year | £79 / year | €89 / year |

Every plan includes:
- 5 devices connected at the same time
- Unlimited data
- All server locations
- Windows, macOS, Android, and iPhone/iPad

You are charged in your local currency where available (GBP or EUR); everywhere else, in USD.

## Free trial

There is no free trial.

## How to pay

- **On the website (all platforms):** go to overleap.io/pricing, choose a plan, and pay by card. Apple Pay and Google Pay are also offered at checkout when your device supports them.
- **On iPhone/iPad:** the Yearly plan can be bought in the app through the App Store. The Monthly plan is only available on overleap.io.
- **On Android:** the app has no in-app purchase. Subscribe on overleap.io, then sign in to the app with the same email address.

We do not accept cryptocurrency, PayPal, or bank transfer.

## Promo codes

If you have a promo code, enter it on the checkout page when paying on overleap.io.

## Renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for.

- Bought on overleap.io: manage or cancel at overleap.io → Account → "Manage subscription".
- Bought through the App Store: manage or cancel in your Apple ID subscription settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).

## After paying

"Payment received. Your subscription will be active within a few minutes — refresh this page to check." Then sign in to the app with the same email address.
