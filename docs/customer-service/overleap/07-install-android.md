# Installing on Android

Requirements: Android 7.0 or later.

## Steps

1. Open the **Google Play Store** and search for **Overleap**.
2. Tap **Install**.
3. Open Overleap and sign in with your email and the code we send you.
4. Tap **Connect**. The first time, Android asks "Connection request — Overleap wants to set up a VPN connection". Tap **OK**.

## Subscribing on Android

The Android app has no in-app purchase. Subscribe at overleap.io (card, Google Pay, or Apple Pay at checkout), then sign in to the app with the same email address.

## I tapped "Cancel" on the VPN prompt

Just tap **Connect** again — the prompt appears again.

## "Another VPN is always on"

If another VPN app is set as "Always-on VPN", Android won't let Overleap connect. Open Settings → Network & internet → VPN, tap the gear next to the other VPN, and turn off "Always-on VPN".

## Updates

Updates come through the Google Play Store.
