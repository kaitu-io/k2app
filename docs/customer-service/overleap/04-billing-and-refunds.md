# Billing & Refunds

## Receipts and invoices

A receipt is emailed for every charge. Invoices can be downloaded from overleap.io → Account → "Manage subscription".

## Changing your card or cancelling

overleap.io → Account → "Manage subscription". There you can update your card, cancel, or see past invoices.

If you subscribed through the App Store, use your Apple ID subscription settings instead (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).

## Refunds

Refund requests are handled by our support team, case by case.
- The assistant must NOT promise or deny a refund.
- Collect the user's account email, then hand off to a human.
- App Store purchases are refunded by Apple: the user can request it at reportaproblem.apple.com. Still hand off if they need help.

## Unexpected charge / charged twice

Hand off to a human. Ask for the account email and the date of the charge first.

## Payment failed

1. Try another card, or Apple Pay / Google Pay at checkout.
2. Make sure the card allows online/international payments.
3. If it still fails, hand off to a human.
