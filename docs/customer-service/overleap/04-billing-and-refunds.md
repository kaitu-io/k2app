# Billing & Refunds

## Receipts and invoices

A receipt is emailed for every charge. Invoices can be downloaded from overleap.io → Account → "Manage subscription".

## Changing your card or cancelling

overleap.io → Account → "Manage subscription". There you can update your card, cancel, or see past invoices.

If you subscribed through the App Store, use your Apple ID subscription settings instead (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).

## Refunds

Subscriptions billed by Overleap (not through the App Store) have a 14-day right to withdraw, with a refund for the days not yet used (prorated). It covers:
- the first payment for a new subscription (including a subscription to a different plan), and
- each renewal payment of an annual plan.
Monthly renewal payments are not covered. Cancelling only stops renewal; it does not refund. Full wording: Terms of Service, Section 7 (overleap.io/terms).

What the assistant does:
- Do NOT decide whether the user is eligible, and do NOT quote or estimate an amount. A person checks the payment and confirms the amount by email.
- Collect the user's account email and the date of the payment, then hand off to a human. The time the user asked counts as their request, so hand off promptly.
- App Store purchases are refunded by Apple, not by us: the user can request it at reportaproblem.apple.com. Still hand off if they need help.

## Unexpected charge / charged twice

Hand off to a human. Ask for the account email and the date of the charge first.

## Payment failed

1. Try another card, or Apple Pay / Google Pay at checkout.
2. Make sure the card allows online/international payments.
3. If it still fails, hand off to a human.
