# Decisions

The standing decisions for the current product. Append only: a new decision gets the next number, and says which earlier one it replaces.

David's words are translated here. The original Chinese wording, and every earlier decision that has since been overturned (the first 41 entries, 2026-10-15 to 2026-11-10), is preserved unchanged in `research/decisions-log-zh-2026-10-15_2026-11-10.md`. The "Origin" column points to entry numbers in that log.

Terms in capitals are defined in `glossary.md`.

## Superseded and still open
- "One primary and two backup Boxes per Guest per country" (log #17) predates the Marketplace, where a Guest picks one Box. What happens when that Box goes offline is open (`open-questions.md`).
- Identity Verification of every Guest through a vendor (D-10, D-21) and the three-level wallet proposal (D-37) are replaced by D-39.
- Flat $7.99 pricing, peer exchange with credits, ESP32 and arm64 hardware, email-only sign-up, and "no logging of visited sites" are all overturned. See the log.
